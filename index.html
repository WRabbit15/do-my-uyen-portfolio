Ogilvy Global Marketing Campaign Performance Analytics

Tools: RStudio, data cleaning, descriptive analytics, visualization

Problem: Ogilvy had global campaign data across cities, channels, customer groups and languages, making it difficult to identify where marketing value was created.

What I did:

Predictive & Prescriptive Analytics

IntelliAuto Employee Turnover Reduction Strategy

Tools: RStudio, logistic regression, Excel Solver, HR analytics

Problem: IntelliAuto faced early-turnover risk during an Industry 4.0 transition and needed to prioritize retention actions under limited training hours and compensation budget.

What I did:

  • Used logistic regression to estimate departure probability for 750 employees.
  • Identified employees with higher predicted early-leave probability.
  • Applied Excel Solver to allocate seminars and salary incentives.
  • Compared 10% and 40% reduction targets to evaluate feasibility.

Outcome: Recommended a feasible 10% turnover-reduction plan and showed that a 40% target required broader HR interventions and additional resources.

Product lens: predict who is likely to churn, then spend a fixed budget where it retains the most.
